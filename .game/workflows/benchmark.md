# Benchmark workflow

Use only when the project has meaningful performance budgets or regression risk.

## Flow

1. Read `docs/PERFORMANCE.md` and identify the exact metric/environment.
2. Record build/commit/runtime/platform/hardware relevant to the result.
3. Use a repeatable benchmark or measurement path.
4. Capture baseline and current result without hiding variance or failed runs.
5. Compare against project-defined thresholds, not a universal OGF number.
6. Report regression/improvement and confidence limits where relevant.
7. Do not infer gameplay feel or real-user device quality from synthetic benchmarks alone.
