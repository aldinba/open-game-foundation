# Product review perspective

Optional read-only review before or during substantial feature work.

Ask:

- What player/user problem does this solve?
- Is the scope small enough to validate?
- What behavior is explicitly out of scope?
- Which assumptions are being made?
- What human evidence would demonstrate value?
- Is there a simpler version that tests the same hypothesis?

Output concrete blockers, assumptions and decisions rather than broad brainstorming.
