# Game design review perspective

Optional review of gameplay/system changes.

Ask:

- What player decision or skill expression changes?
- Which existing gameplay invariants are affected?
- Are rules, feedback and failure states understandable?
- Could this create dominant, degenerate or frustrating behavior?
- What must be playtested rather than inferred from code/tests?
- Does the change require an update to `docs/GAMEPLAY.md`?
